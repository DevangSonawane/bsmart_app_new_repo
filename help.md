Help
How do I earn BCoins?
You can earn Coins by:

● Watching eligible videos or content.
● Participating in campaigns, challenges, or events.
● Completing tasks and activities within the app.
● Receiving promotional or bonus rewards during special events.
 
How do I redeem bCoins?
● Go to Vault.
● Tap Redeem Gift Cards.
● Choose your preferred gift card.
● Select required Card Value
● Ensure you have the required number of Coins.
● Confirm the redemption request.
● Your redemption status can be tracked under My Redemptions.
 
Why is my bCoin balance different?
Your balance may change because:

● Coins were successfully redeemed.
● New Coins were added after completing eligible activities.
● Promotional or bonus Coins expired.
● A transaction is still being processed.
Check Transaction History for complete details.

 
How long does redemption take?
Most redemption requests are processed within 2–72 hours. Processing time may vary depending on the reward provider.

 

How will I be notified after my voucher redemption is completed?
Once your voucher redemption is successfully processed, you will be notified through one or more of the following channels, depending on your notification preferences and the service availability:

● In-App Notification
● Email
● SMS (Text Message)
You can also view the redemption status and voucher details anytime by navigating to Wallet & Coins → My Redemptions.

If you do not receive a notification or your voucher within the expected processing time, please contact Support by going to Profile → Settings → Help & Support → Raise a Ticket.

 
Where can I check my redemption status?
Open My Redemptions to view:

● Pending requests
● Processing requests
● Successful redemptions
● Failed or cancelled requests
 
Do bCoins expire?
Some promotional or bonus Coins may have an expiry date. Please check the campaign or offer details to know the validity period.

 
Can I cancel a redemption request?
Once a redemption request has been submitted and is being processed, it cannot usually be cancelled. Contact support if you need assistance.

 
Why can't I redeem my bCoins?
Possible reasons include:

● Insufficient bCoins balance.
● Temporary service maintenance.
● Reward currently unavailable.
● Account verification is incomplete.
● Daily or monthly redemption limit has been reached.
 
Is there a minimum number of bCoins required for redemption?
Yes. Each gift card has its own minimum Coin requirement, which is displayed before you redeem.

 
How can I contact Support?
If you experience any issues related to earning Coins, redeeming rewards, or wallet transactions, you can contact our support team by following these steps:

Support Process:

1. Go to Profile.
2. Open Settings.
3. Navigate to Help & Support.
4. Select Raise a Ticket or Contact Support.
5. Submit your issue with the required details, and our support team will review and assist you as soon as possible.
 
Tips
● Complete your profile to unlock more earning opportunities.
● Check the app regularly for bonus campaigns and limited-time offers.
● Review your Transaction History to keep track of earned and redeemed Coins.
● Ensure your account details are up to date before redeeming rewards.
● Do not share your account credentials with anyone.
● Follow the app's Terms & Conditions to avoid forfeiture of Coins due to policy violations.
